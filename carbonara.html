Spaghetti Carbonara

Description

Spaghetti Carbonara is a classic Italian pasta dish made with eggs, cheese, pancetta, and black pepper. Creamy, simple, and delicious.

Ingredients

  • 400g spaghetti
  • 150g pancetta or bacon
  • 2 large eggs
  • 50g parmesan cheese (grated)
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • Salt and black pepper

Steps

  1. Cook spaghetti in salted boiling water until al dente.
  2. Fry pancetta with garlic until crispy.
  3. Whisk eggs and parmesan together in a bowl.
  4. Drain pasta and add it to the pancetta pan.
  5. Remove from heat and quickly stir in egg mixture.
  6. Season with black pepper and serve immediately.
