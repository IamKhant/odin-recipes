Lasagna

Description

Homemade lasagna isn't as hard to make as it seems. Layers of rich meat sauce, creamy cheese, and tender pasta come together to create a classic comfort food everyone loves.

Ingredients

  1. 500g ground beef
  2. 1 medium onion (chopped)
  3. 2 cloves garlic (minced)
  4. 800g tomato sauce
  5. 2 tablespoons tomato paste
  6. Salt and pepper (to taste)
  7. 1 teaspoon dried oregano
  8. Lasagna noodles
  9. 250g ricotta cheese
  10. 200g mozzarella cheese (shredded)
  11. 50g parmesan cheese (grated)

Steps

  • Preheat the oven to 180°C (350°F).
  • Cook the lasagna noodles according to package instructions.
  • In a pan, cook ground beef until browned. Add onion and garlic, then cook until soft.
  • Stir in tomato sauce, tomato paste, salt, pepper, and oregano. Simmer for 15 minutes.
  • Spread a thin layer of meat sauce in a baking dish.
  • Layer noodles, ricotta cheese, meat sauce, and mozzarella cheese.
  • Repeat layers until ingredients are used, finishing with mozzarella and parmesan on top.
  • Bake for 35–40 minutes until bubbly and golden.
  • Let rest for 10 minutes before serving.
